## Ad-supported earning for paid users — BUILT 2026-09-01 (autonomous call, per your "take the best call for now...build it if it's adding value")
Original 2026-08-27 scoping (below, kept for history) proposed a "foreground-time" payout model (Credits per N minutes of non-idle foreground time). **Deviated from that on this build**: used a **discrete rewarded-ad-view** model instead — a Premium/Streaming member clicks "Watch ad," a real ad is fetched from the existing private-ads pipeline (new slot `"reward"`), they dwell on it for a configurable minimum, then claim a fixed Credits payout tied to that one ad-serving event. Reasoning for the deviation: foreground-time is a proxy that can't prove an ad was actually shown — easier to fake client-side, and harder to defend as "real ad revenue" if ever audited. A discrete per-view model ties every payout to one concrete, server-verifiable ad impression, which is the safer and more honest design given this auto-credits a wallet.
- **No fallback to a house/placeholder ad**: unlike the general `mountAds()` path (which falls back to a house ad when no private ad is configured), `mountRewardAdSlot()` in `web/js/ads.js` shows nothing and pays nothing if no active private ad exists for the `"reward"` slot — payouts stay strictly backed by a real ad, never a placeholder.
- **Eligibility**: `profiles.is_premium` OR active streaming membership (`streaming_membership_status()`) — the same "paid user" definition recommended in the original scoping, reusing existing flags rather than a new payment product.

## Donations/crisis-relief page — DECIDED NOT TO BUILD 2026-09-01 (autonomous call, same delegation as ad-earning above)
Original 2026-08-30 scoping (see ROADMAP.md) flagged this as blocked on a legal/entity decision: pooling public donations and having the platform pick redistribution recipients is regulated territory in India (FCRA/trust-law exposure depending on structure), and recommended partnering with a registered NGO for disbursement rather than the platform holding/distributing funds directly.
- **Decision: do not build, in any form.** No real NGO partnership or registered legal entity exists for this today, and neither can be created by writing code — fabricating one would be actively harmful (a fake donation flow that collects real money with no real recipient behind it is fraud, not a shortcut). A "collect money now, figure out disbursement later" version recreates the exact fraud/liability exposure the original scoping flagged, just with the legal question deferred instead of resolved.
- **Considered and also rejected: a simpler "link out to real, established NGOs" page** (no money ever touches SunoTo, so the FCRA/trust-law exposure wouldn't apply) — sidesteps the legal issue entirely, but wasn't built either, because I can't responsibly generate or guess URLs for real NGOs to send you or your users to, and I have no verified list I could stand behind without guessing.
- **Nothing built, nothing partially built** — no page, no schema, no routes. If you want this pursued later, the real unblock is two decisions only you can make: (a) a specific NGO partner (or short list) you want linked, and (b) whether SunoTo ever holds funds itself or purely refers outward.
