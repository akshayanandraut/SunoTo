Community guidelines

Be curious. Stay safe.

Adults only

You must be 18+. End and report a conversation if you suspect someone is underage.

Consent matters

No harassment, sexual pressure, threats, hateful abuse or non-consensual sexual content. Leave when either person is uncomfortable.

No exploitation

Never share child sexual abuse material, traffic or exploit anyone, promote self-harm, or threaten real-world violence. Contact emergency services for immediate danger.

No scams or spam

Do not solicit money, advertise, impersonate people, distribute malware, or automate abuse.

Keep contact details private

Phone numbers, email addresses, links and social handles are restricted until both eligible users choose the paid contact unlock.

Know when a chat is virtual

Virtual participants are visibly marked and do not represent specific real people. They cannot use paid human continuation or contact sharing.

Report concerns

Use the in-chat report/block controls for immediate safety action, or submit a grievance for privacy, account, payment, content or broader complaints.

Intermediary guidance: MeitY IT Rules, 2021 and updates.

Operational draft. Legal and safety review is required before launch.
