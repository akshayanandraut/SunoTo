Plain-language privacy

Your normal chats are not a permanent server archive.

Normal chat messages are relayed and are not permanently stored by our servers. History and favourites are kept in your browser and may disappear when you clear browser data or use private browsing.

Data the service needs

We process account email, pseudonymous identity and abuse signals, temporary matching state, payment and wallet records, safety reports, blocks, grievances and content-free aggregate product analytics. Radius matching uses precise location temporarily; peers receive at most a coarse distance band.

Virtual chats

A clearly marked virtual participant may process your latest message through the configured AI provider to reply. Virtual chats do not create a permanent server transcript and never incur the human paid-message continuation fee.

Retention

Temporary room and matching state expires when no longer needed. Analytics deduplication keys are scheduled for removal after 35 days. Resolved grievance records are scheduled for removal after three years. Payment, wallet and audit records may be retained for fraud prevention, accounting or other legal obligations.

Your controls

Signed-in users can download a server data export and request account deletion from the Account page. A deletion request immediately prevents new matching while the request is processed. Local chat history must be cleared separately in this browser. You can also submit a grievance.

India privacy legislation: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

Operational draft, not legal advice. Counsel must approve the controller identity, lawful notices, retention periods and effective date before launch.
