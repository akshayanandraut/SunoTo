Plain-language privacy

Your chats stay on this device.

Normal chat messages are not permanently stored by our servers. Local history and favourites are kept in your browser and may disappear in private browsing.

Data the service needs

We may process account email, IP and risk signals, temporary location for radius matching, payment records, reports, blocks and product analytics. Exact GPS is not shown to other people and is not kept as permanent location history.

Virtual chats

If you match with a clearly marked virtual participant, your latest message may be processed by the configured AI provider to create a reply. The server does not retain a virtual chat transcript as permanent account data.

Your controls

You can clear local conversations and favourites from the product. An account deletion and grievance route will be provided before production launch.

Draft for product development. Legal review is required before launch.
